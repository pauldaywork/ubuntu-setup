ModAlt + letter: every command
+ Ctrl: the same key's companion (carry, list, rename…)
+ Shift: toward another monitor
herdr: prefix, then key
Press Ctrl+B, let go, then the key: prefix▸V splits side by side.
Inside herdr, prefix▸? lists what's bound right now.
